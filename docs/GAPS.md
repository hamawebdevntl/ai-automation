# Gap analysis — stack vs. requirements (2026-08-30)

Verdict: the stack covers **production (stages 4-9) and publishing (11-12) well**.
The gaps cluster in three places: platform quotas that cap 10/day, trend research
for the two platforms that matter most, and compliance/QC that nobody has written.

---

## A. Hard blockers on "10 Reels/day" — platform-side, no repo fixes these

### A1. YouTube API quota caps you at 6 uploads/day, not 10
YouTube Data API v3 grants **10,000 units/day** by default. `videos.insert`
costs **1,600 units**. That is **6 uploads/day** before you hit the wall — and
the 7th fails, it does not queue.

Fix: request a quota extension. It requires a compliance audit and takes weeks.
**This is critical path and nobody has started it.** Alternative for the interim:
accept that YouTube gets 6 of the 10 and the rest go elsewhere.

### A2. TikTok automated posting requires an audited app
Confirmed in `tiktok.provider.ts:28`: `content_posting_method=UPLOAD` **does not
publish**. It drops media into the user's TikTok inbox where a human must finish
it manually within 24 hours or it is discarded. Line 182 adds: **"TikTok limits
pending posts to 5 within any 24-hour period"** in that mode.

So unattended publishing *requires* `DIRECT_POST`, and DIRECT_POST requires an
audited TikTok app. Unaudited apps are restricted to private/self-only posts.
Audit is 2-4 weeks. Also critical path.

### A3. Instagram is fine, with prerequisites
~50 posts/24h — well above 10. Needs an Instagram Business/Creator account
linked to a Facebook Page. Note the API cannot attach licensed music to a Reel;
audio must be baked into the render.

## B. Real gaps in the stack

### B1. TikTok and Instagram trend research is barely covered
This is the weakest part of the whole design, and it is stage 1 — the input to
everything else.
- ViralMint has **no TikTok trend implementation**. `tiktok_scout` is
  cookie-based and routes through `_scout_via_ytdlp_search` (yt-dlp), not an
  API. Cookie auth breaks constantly.
- ViralMint has **no Instagram trend source at all** — one passing string
  reference in `scout.py` and nothing behind it.
- Our TikTok-Api fallback is 1 author, 12 commits/year, scraping a platform
  that actively fights scrapers.

What genuinely works today: `youtube_scout.py`, `google_trends_svc.py`, and
`trend_velocity_service.py` (the baseline-relative velocity scoring, which is
the good part). So **trend research is really YouTube + Google Trends**, and we
should say so rather than pretend TikTok/Instagram are covered.

### B2. QC is technical only — there is no factual or brand-safety check
OpenMontage's QC validates ffprobe output, audio levels, subtitle presence and
slideshow risk. All of that is about whether the *file* is good. Nothing checks
whether the *claims* are true, on-brand, or safe to publish under your
organization's name. For a non-profit this is the higher risk of the two.

### B3. AI-content disclosure is wired for TikTok only
`tiktok.provider.ts:553` sets `is_aigc` from a `video_made_with_ai` setting —
good, and we must set it. But YouTube requires disclosing altered/synthetic
content, and Meta labels AI content on Instagram. Those are not handled.

Separately: **EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations were scheduled to
apply from 2 August 2026** — i.e. already in force as of today, if you have EU
nexus. My knowledge cutoff is May 2026 and there was active discussion of
delaying parts of the Act, so **verify the current position with counsel rather
than taking my word for it.**

### B4. Per-platform copy variants are not generated by anything
Postiz accepts different content per integration, but nothing in the stack
*writes* those variants. A TikTok caption, a YouTube title + description, a
LinkedIn post and an Instagram caption are four different pieces of writing for
the same reel. This is a custom generation step we have not budgeted.

### B5. No brand kit
Logos, fonts, colour tokens, lower-third templates, mandatory disclaimers,
approved voice. Remotion compositions need these as inputs and nothing holds them.

### B6. No idempotency / dedup
Nothing prevents regenerating the same idea, or double-posting on a retry.
At 300 reels/month with retries, this will bite.

**Largely closed since.** `productions_one_live_per_idea` refuses a second live
production per idea; `claim_render_slot` and `claim_publish_slot` are
conditional updates that make the two irreversible steps single-shot even when
two workers hold the same row; HeyGen submits carry an `Idempotency-Key`; and
publish is never retried, by design, because Postiz starts its workflow with
`TERMINATE_EXISTING`. What is left is the fal lane, which has no idempotency key
of its own -- an ambiguous fal failure parks rather than resubmitting.

### B7. Reviewer throughput is unmodelled
Two gates times 10 reels a day is 20 review actions daily, every day. If the
owner is away for three days the queue is 60 items deep. Needs batch-approve,
auto-expiry, or a delegated second reviewer — a product decision, not a technical one.

## C. Deferred, but name them now
Observability and alerting; a retention policy for the Supabase Storage
renders bucket; voice-cloning consent if a real person's voice is used; music
licensing for organizational use; analytics-to-idea-scoring loop (already known
custom).

**The fleet-level cost ceiling is no longer deferred.** It was named here as
missing -- "OpenMontage's cost_tracker is per-project, not per-month" -- and
`20260908140000_spend_caps.sql` closes it: a USD ceiling per provider and per
model, on a daily *and* a monthly window, with a ledger recording what every
render actually cost behind it. Enforced twice, at Gate 1 and again at the
render step, from one verdict function so the two cannot disagree.

---

## What actually changes in the plan
1. **Start YouTube quota extension AND TikTok audit this week.** Both are
   multi-week, both are critical path, neither has begun.
2. **Re-scope stage 1 honestly** to YouTube + Google Trends, and treat
   TikTok/Instagram trend data as a research spike rather than a solved input.
3. **Add a claims/brand-safety gate** to QC before Gate 2.
4. **Add per-platform copy generation** as an explicit pipeline stage.
